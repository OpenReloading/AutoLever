# Wiring

!!! danger "Safety"

    --8<-- "safety-short.md"

    The full warning is on the [introduction page](index.md).

Red wires carry +24 V and black wires carry Ground (0 V). A sensor keeps its own wires: brown, blue and black. Pin and terminal numbers are the ones printed on the part; each step lists the connectors of its parts with their wire colours.
