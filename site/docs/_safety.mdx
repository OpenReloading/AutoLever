Moving parts can cause serious injury. Keep hands clear while the press is powered or pressurised. Before reaching in, making adjustments or clearing a jam, unplug the power supply, close the air shut-off and release all remaining air pressure. E-STOP is not a safety-rated emergency stop. This project uses 24 V DC only, from an enclosed, CE-marked external power adapter. Household electricity (230 V) can kill. Never connect 230 V directly to the project or open or modify the adapter. Leave electrical wiring and repairs to a qualified electrician. This is a DIY reference design whose safety has not been independently verified. Have your build checked by a suitably qualified person before use and follow local safety requirements. Do not use it for priming.
